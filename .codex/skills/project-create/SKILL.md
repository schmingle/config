# Create a project

Run on explicit human invocation. Creating or reviewing this configuration does not invoke it. Read [the shared project contract and alignment rules](references/project-contract.md) before discovery or drafting. Keep the durable project contract, decisions, authority provenance, and handoff in Linear so task-history loss does not prevent resumption; native tasks supply execution context. Keep no local registry or project-management files.

1. **Discover before proposing a new project.** Recover the intended outcome and known decisions from the conversation and linked sources, verify the intended Linear workspace/account, and inspect relevant existing projects and tickets. Prefer reusing a suitable project; propose a separate finite version only when its outcome and boundary justify one. Resolve duplicate candidates or ambiguous destinations before writes; do not create a second project merely because its name differs.
2. **Interview only for consequential gaps.** Ask one meaningful question at a time, starting with the decision that most affects the project. Offer numbered choices ordered from the recommended option, clearly labeled, with concrete tradeoffs; accept a freeform answer. Use established decisions and evidence instead of reasking. With clear inputs, proceed to a draft without a mandatory questionnaire. Cover motivation and intended outcome, this version's scope/exclusions, decisions versus assumptions/open questions, observable KRs, completion proof, phases and exits, dependencies/parallel work, and guardrails. Do not invent business requirements, metrics, dates, ownership, or approvals to fill gaps.
3. **Make the proposal reviewable.** Draft the contract using the canonical template linked from the shared reference, plus proposed initial native milestones and implementation tickets. Show how the work covers the KRs, phase exits, and validation, with a practical initial next action. Identify existing records to reuse, unresolved decisions, and any proposed reorganization or ownership changes. Do not expand a continuing workstream into an unbounded project.
4. **Apply the authorized proposal.** Obtain approval before creating the project or reorganizing existing work unless the human has already specifically approved those actions and this concrete scope. Do not ask again for covered decisions. Apply only the approved portion; hold consequential unresolved changes while continuing safe preparation. Preserve existing owners, relationships, decision provenance, and historical context. For reuse or reorganization, apply the shared in-flight safeguards: approval alone does not prove work has settled, and unresolved effects on active owners/batches hold the affected proposal while safe independent preparation continues.
5. **Verify and hand off.** Read back the project, milestones, and tickets, checking their actual contents and bindings. Recover partial or ambiguous creation responses through discovery/readback before retrying; retain successful records rather than starting over. Publish an initial project summary for creation or material tracking changes only when permitted by the [shared reporting policy](../../AGENTS.md#project-status-updates). Preserve any maintained supervision coordination document separately; do not append its contents to the summary. For unchanged reuse or a draft-only/blocked proposal, report in chat without a project update. Report what was created or reused, what remains draft or blocked, and whether the contract is ready for implementation.

Do not start implementation, supervision, tasks, or recurring schedules as a side effect. Another explicit-only skill requires its own human invocation; readiness is not authorization to invoke it.
